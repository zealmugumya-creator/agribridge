AgriBridge Transporter Agreement

Version 1.0.1 · Effective 9 November 2026

Status of this document. These terms apply to all individuals and businesses registered on AgriBridge as transporters or delivery drivers. They have not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate. Where something is marked as requiring confirmation, we will publish a new version and ask you to review it if the change is material. Nothing here removes rights you have under Ugandan law.

These extra terms apply to you in addition to the AgriBridge Terms of Use. If there is a conflict, these extra terms prevail for transporter-specific matters.

1. Who this agreement applies to

This agreement applies to any individual or business that registers on AgriBridge as a transporter, delivery driver, or logistics provider, whether registered directly or invited by an administrator. You must be 18 or older. If you register on behalf of a business, you confirm you have authority to bind that business.

2. Eligibility and registration

To register as a transporter you must:

  • Provide accurate personal details including your legal name, phone number and national identity information as required.
  • Provide accurate vehicle information including vehicle type, registration plate and condition.
  • Hold a valid driving licence and any other permits required by Ugandan law for the vehicle type and cargo you will carry.
  • Not be disqualified from driving or carrying goods for hire under Ugandan law.

AgriBridge may verify your identity and vehicle details before or after registration. Providing false information is grounds for immediate suspension and may be referred to relevant authorities.

3. Your status

You are an independent contractor, not an employee, agent or partner of AgriBridge. AgriBridge provides a platform that connects you with delivery opportunities. AgriBridge does not direct the manner in which you perform deliveries, does not provide your vehicle, fuel or equipment, and does not guarantee a minimum volume of work. You are responsible for all costs associated with your vehicle and operations.

Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship. You are responsible for your own tax obligations under Ugandan law, including income tax and any applicable VAT or withholding requirements. Tax obligations: to be confirmed with a qualified accountant or the Uganda Revenue Authority.

4. Delivery standards

When you accept a delivery assignment on AgriBridge, you agree to:

  • Collect the goods from the agreed pick-up location at the agreed time, or as soon as practicable after confirmation.
  • Handle agricultural produce and goods with care to prevent damage, spoilage or contamination.
  • Deliver to the address shown in the order within the agreed timeframe.
  • Obtain proof of delivery as required by the platform (photograph, recipient signature or confirmation code).
  • Contact the buyer and AgriBridge promptly if you encounter a problem with collection or delivery.
  • Not transfer a delivery to another person without AgriBridge's prior approval.
  • Not consume, sell or otherwise misuse any goods entrusted to you for delivery.

5. Food safety and hygiene

Agricultural produce and food items must be carried in a clean, hygienic vehicle or compartment appropriate for the goods. You must comply with the Uganda National Bureau of Standards requirements and any other applicable food safety rules for the goods you carry. AgriBridge may suspend your account if a complaint about hygiene or handling is upheld.

6. Tracking and location data

AgriBridge collects your device location during active delivery assignments to provide buyers with delivery tracking and to help verify delivery completion. Location data is not collected continuously outside active assignments. Location data is shared with the buyer for the order it relates to and retained as described in the Privacy Policy. You must enable location access on your device during active assignments to use the platform. The lawful basis for processing delivery location data is contract performance. Where we process location data for safety, fraud prevention or compliance, the basis is legitimate interests or legal obligation.

7. Identity and photograph

AgriBridge may require and display your name, photograph and vehicle details to buyers for the purpose of allowing them to identify you at pick-up or delivery. This is necessary for the performance of the delivery contract. By registering, you consent to your name, photograph and relevant vehicle details being displayed to buyers in connection with their orders. You may update your photograph through your account settings.

8. Payment

Delivery fees are set by AgriBridge or by negotiation as described in the platform. Payment is made via the method set up in your transporter account. Payment is processed after delivery is confirmed. AgriBridge may deduct any applicable platform commission before remitting your payment. The applicable rate will be shown to you before you accept an assignment.

AgriBridge is not responsible for delays caused by your mobile money provider or bank. If you believe a payment is incorrect, contact AgriBridge within 14 days of the delivery date. Dispute resolution process: to be confirmed.

9. Cancellation and no-shows

Accepting an assignment and then failing to collect without reasonable notice harms buyers and sellers. Repeated unexplained cancellations or no-shows may result in your account being suspended or removed. If you need to cancel an accepted assignment, do so as early as possible through the platform and contact AgriBridge to explain.

10. Insurance

You are responsible for holding any insurance required by Ugandan law for your vehicle and operations, including third-party motor vehicle insurance. AgriBridge does not provide insurance for you, your vehicle, or the goods in transit. Insurance requirements: to be confirmed with a Ugandan insurance broker and legal adviser.

11. Liability for loss or damage

If goods are lost, damaged or spoiled as a result of your negligence or failure to meet the delivery standards in section 4, AgriBridge may deduct the value of the loss from a future payment or require you to reimburse the affected party. Liability limits and the dispute resolution process for damage claims will be set out in the AgriBridge Delivery and Dispute Resolution Policy. Liability limits: to be confirmed.

12. Prohibited conduct

You must not:

  • Use AgriBridge to solicit direct business that bypasses the platform in breach of these terms.
  • Harass, threaten or discriminate against buyers, sellers or AgriBridge staff.
  • Drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Carry unauthorised passengers or additional cargo in a way that affects the safety or integrity of the delivery.
  • Misrepresent your identity or vehicle details.
  • Share your account with another person.

13. Suspension and termination

AgriBridge may suspend or close your transporter account for a material breach of these terms, serious misconduct, a pattern of poor performance or complaints, failure to maintain required licences, or if required by law. Where possible, we will give you notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond before taking action. In cases of serious misconduct or fraud, we may suspend immediately.

14. Changes to this agreement

AgriBridge will give you reasonable notice of material changes to this agreement. If you continue to accept assignments after a material change takes effect, you are taken to have accepted the new terms. For non-material corrections (such as fixing a typographical error), no new acceptance is required.

15. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of Uganda. Dispute resolution mechanism: to be confirmed with a qualified Ugandan legal adviser.

16. Contact

For questions about this agreement or delivery operations, contact AgriBridge at agribrige@gmail.com. For payment disputes, use the dispute process in your account settings.
