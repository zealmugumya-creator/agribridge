AgriBridge Buyer Terms

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 9 October 2026

These terms apply in addition to the AgriBridge Terms of Use if you register as a buyer, including a hotel or restaurant. They have not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate.

1. Accurate order information

Give correct names, phone numbers and delivery details. Check the product, quantity and delivery area before you confirm. If you buy for a business, you confirm you are allowed to do so.

2. Prices and charges

You will see the price in UGX and any delivery or service fee before you confirm. The amount shown is what you will be asked to pay for that order. Prices and stock may change before an order is confirmed.

3. Payment

Pay using one of the methods offered. An order may remain pending until payment is confirmed by the payment provider and verified by AgriBridge. Do not pay anyone outside the instructions AgriBridge gives you for your order.

4. Business accounts and invoices

Hotels and restaurants may ask for invoicing. Credit or payment terms are only available if AgriBridge has agreed them with you in writing. Any late-payment charge must be agreed in advance and shown to you; none applies automatically under these terms.

5. Delivery or collection

Be available to receive or collect goods at the agreed time. Check the goods on receipt and tell us quickly about any problem.

6. Cancellations and complaints

You may cancel a pending order from your order page, or by contacting us. Once an order is confirmed or in transit, cancellation may not be possible or may be subject to conditions we will explain. If goods are not as described, damaged or not delivered, contact agribrige@gmail.com or +256 755 966 690 with your order reference and photos, ideally within 48 hours. This is a guide and does not limit your consumer rights under Ugandan law.

7. Respectful and lawful communication

Be courteous to sellers and staff. Do not harass, threaten, or use a seller's details for anything other than the order. Do not try to deal outside AgriBridge to avoid fees.

8. Your consumer rights

Nothing in these terms removes any right you have under the law as a consumer.
