**Version 1.0.0 · Effective 10 November 2026**

> **Status of this document.** This policy describes what AgriBridge stores in your browser today. It has not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate. Where something is "to be confirmed", we will publish a new version and tell you if the change is material. Nothing here removes rights you have under Ugandan law.

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage used by https://agribrige.com and the AgriBridge app. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data on our servers under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

## 2. What we actually store

AgriBridge does **not** use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or marketing pixels. We have checked the code that runs in your browser and none are present. What the app does use is your browser's local storage, which works like a cookie but stays on your device:

- **Sign-in and security:** your session token, your account type, and a count of recent sign-in attempts used to slow down password guessing.
- **Preferences:** your language, whether you have seen the welcome screen, and whether you dismissed the install or notification prompts.
- **Marketplace state:** your cart and orders cache, a profile you started but did not finish, and your cookie choice itself.
- **Offline support:** the app's service worker caches pages and images so the app can open offline and receive push notifications you asked for.

These items are strictly necessary or functional. They are not used to build an advertising profile of you, and they are not shared with advertisers.

## 3. Essential and optional categories

Today every item above is **essential or functional**. There are no optional tracking categories, so there is nothing to opt into. The cookie banner records your choice, but no non-essential storage is loaded whether you accept or decline, because none exists.

If we ever add an optional category (for example, anonymised usage analytics), we will update this policy first and ask for your affirmative consent **before** any optional storage is set. Continuing to browse, or signing in with Google, will never be treated as consent to optional cookies.

## 4. Third parties

Some services that AgriBridge relies on may set their own cookies or storage:

- **Our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare)** may set strictly necessary cookies, for example to detect bots and keep the site available. The exact inventory is **to be confirmed** from the host configuration.
- **Payment providers (Pesapal and Flutterwave)** set their own cookies when you visit their checkout pages to pay. Those are governed by their own policies, not this one.
- **Google**, if you choose Google sign-in, is governed by Google's own privacy terms.

We do not control third-party cookies and we do not use them to track you across other websites.

## 5. Managing your preferences

You can clear or block browser storage in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential storage will stop sign-in, your cart and your preferences from working, because the app needs them to function. Signing out clears your session from the device. Acknowledging this policy does not opt you into optional tracking.

## 6. Server-side records are separate

AgriBridge keeps server-side records of orders, payments and meaningful account activity to run the marketplace and keep it safe. Those are personal data handled under our Privacy Policy, not cookies, and this policy does not change how they are kept.

## 7. Changes to this policy

Each version has an effective date and older versions are kept. A material change will be published as a new version and brought to your attention.
