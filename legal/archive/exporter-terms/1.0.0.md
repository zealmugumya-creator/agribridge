# AgriBridge Exporter and Trading Company Terms

**Version 1.0.0 · Effective 8 November 2026**

These terms apply in addition to the AgriBridge Terms of Use if you register as an exporter or trading company. They have not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate.

## 1. Accurate listings

Describe the goods you offer truthfully: type, variety, grade, quantity, unit, price in UGX, country of origin, packing and photos. Use your own photos or ones you have the right to use, and say what they show. Do not claim organic, certified, inspected or phytosanitary status unless it is true and you can show the documents if asked.

## 2. Stock, packing and shipment

Keep your available quantity and status up to date. State the packing you can actually supply (for example bag or carton sizes) and the shipment period you can actually meet. You must not accept orders for more than you can supply. If your stock, packing or schedule changes after you accepted an order, tell the buyer and AgriBridge straight away.

## 3. Quality, certification and legal compliance

Goods must match your listing and be fit for the purpose you state. You are responsible for complying with the export rules of the country you ship from and the import rules of the destination country, including food safety, plant-health and phytosanitary certificates, packaging and labelling. Do not sell goods treated with chemicals contrary to the label or the law.

## 4. Offers and orders

When AgriBridge sends you an offer or an order, respond within the time shown. Accepting is a commitment to supply the stated product, quantity, quality, packing and shipment period. If you decline or do not respond in time, the offer may go to another seller. Repeated acceptance followed by failure to deliver may lead to a warning, limits on your account, or suspension.

## 5. Fulfilment and communication

Prepare, hand over or ship goods as agreed, and keep the buyer informed of the shipment's progress. Be polite and responsive. Do not use buyer details for anything other than the order.

## 6. Verification and your account

We may ask you to verify your company registration, identity, location or the goods you offer. We may approve, pause or suspend an exporter account while we check, giving reasons where we can. One login is one type of account: if you already hold an AgriBridge account of another type, contact us instead of opening a second one.

## 7. Payment, fees and payouts

AgriBridge may deduct a service fee or commission, which will be shown to you before you accept an order. Payouts are made after the order conditions are met. Payout timing and conditions are to be confirmed.

## 8. Cancellations, complaints, refunds

If an order is cancelled, goods rejected, or a complaint upheld, the order records will be used to decide what is fair, and you may need to refund or replace goods. You may give your side of the story. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law.
