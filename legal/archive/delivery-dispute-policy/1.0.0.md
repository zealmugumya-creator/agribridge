# AgriBridge Delivery and Dispute Resolution Policy

**Version 1.0.0 · Effective 10 November 2026**

> **Status of this document.** This policy describes how delivery and disputes work on AgriBridge today. It has not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate. Where something is "to be confirmed", we will publish a new version and ask you to review it if the change is material. Nothing here removes rights you have under Ugandan law.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every account type and expands sections 11 and 12 of the Terms of Use. Transporters should read it together with the Transporter Agreement, which prevails for transporter-specific matters.

## 2. How delivery is arranged

Delivery or collection is arranged for each order, and any delivery fee is shown before you confirm. A delivery is assigned to a transporter by AgriBridge, or claimed by an eligible transporter. Only one transporter holds a live assignment for an order at a time, so responsibility is clear.

## 3. Tracking and delivery statuses

A delivery moves through these statuses: **scheduled**, **picked up**, **in transit**, optionally **at a checkpoint**, and then **delivered** or **failed**. During an active assignment the transporter's device location is used to provide tracking, as described in the Transporter Agreement and the Privacy Policy. Delivery is completed with proof of delivery — a photograph, the recipient's signature, or a confirmation code.

## 4. Buyer responsibilities

Give an accurate delivery address and contact number, and be available to receive the goods. Inspect the goods at delivery where you can, and raise any visible problem promptly. If you cannot be available, tell us or the seller as early as possible.

## 5. Seller responsibilities

Hand the goods over on time, in the condition described, and packed appropriately for the journey. Keep stock and prices current so an order can actually be fulfilled.

## 6. Transporter responsibilities

Collect and deliver within the agreed timeframe, handle produce and goods with care, keep the vehicle hygienic, obtain proof of delivery, and contact the buyer and AgriBridge promptly if a problem arises. The full duties are in the Transporter Agreement.

## 7. If something goes wrong

If goods are not delivered, arrive damaged, spoiled or not as described, tell us at [agribrige@gmail.com](mailto:agribrige@gmail.com) or on WhatsApp (+256 755 966 690) as soon as you can, **ideally within 48 hours of delivery**, with your order reference and photos where possible. This is a guide to help us investigate while evidence is fresh; it is not a limit on your legal rights.

## 8. How we resolve a dispute

1. **Tell us.** Use the contact above with your order reference.
2. **We gather the records.** We review the order, the delivery status and proof of delivery, the payment record, and the evidence from both sides.
3. **We decide fairly.** We aim to resolve the matter promptly, which may include a replacement or a refund handled under the Payment and Refund Policy.
4. **You keep your legal remedies.** If the matter is not resolved, you may use any remedy the law gives you, including the Ugandan courts and any applicable consumer or regulatory body. This policy does not impose mandatory arbitration.

## 9. Responsibility and liability

AgriBridge is responsible for its own services and for what the law makes it responsible for. A seller or buyer is responsible for their own breach of an order, except as the law provides. Where goods are lost or damaged through a transporter's negligence, the Transporter Agreement applies. **Any further limit on liability, and any compensation scale, are to be confirmed** with a Ugandan advocate and are not included in this version. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 10. Failed deliveries and no-shows

If a delivery fails or a buyer is unavailable, we will work with the parties to re-deliver or cancel the order according to its status and the Payment and Refund Policy. Repeated unexplained transporter cancellations or no-shows may lead to suspension under the Transporter Agreement.
