# AgriBridge Payment and Refund Policy

**Version 1.0.0 · Effective 10 November 2026**

> **Status of this document.** This policy describes how payments and refunds work on AgriBridge today. It has not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate. Where something is "to be confirmed", we will publish a new version and ask you to review it if the change is material. Nothing here removes rights you have under Ugandan law.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every account type and expands section 10 of the Terms of Use. If there is a conflict on a payment matter, the Terms of Use and the law prevail.

## 2. Payment methods

You can pay using the methods shown to you at checkout. The methods the platform supports are:

- **Cash on delivery**, where offered for your order.
- **Direct mobile money to the seller**, where you pay the farmer's or seller's own number.
- **Bank transfer**, which an administrator verifies against our account before the order is marked paid. It is never marked paid automatically.
- **Card and mobile money through a payment provider** (Pesapal or Flutterwave, including MTN MoMo and Airtel Money), when those providers are enabled.

Not every method is available at all times or for every order. We only show you the methods that are live, and we do not guarantee that any particular method will keep working.

## 3. When a payment counts as made

For integrated online payments, a payment is treated as successful only after the payment provider's confirmation has been verified by our server. Bank transfers require administrator verification. For cash or direct payments to the seller, keep evidence and confirm receipt with the seller; these are not automatically verified by an online gateway. A message on your phone, a receipt screen, or an SMS alone does not mark an order paid. Order status and payment status are tracked separately: an order can be confirmed while its payment is still being verified.

## 4. Prices, fees and commission

Prices are in {{CURRENCY}}. The price, and any delivery fee or platform commission that applies to you, is shown before you confirm an order, and that is the amount that applies to that order. We do not add hidden deductions. The applicable commission rate, where one applies, is shown at the point it is charged rather than fixed in this document. **Commission schedule: to be confirmed and published.**

## 5. No escrow

AgriBridge is **not** an escrow provider or a guarantor of every transaction. With direct methods you pay the seller directly. Where payment passes through a payment provider, the provider processes the money and we keep a record of the transaction and of any settlement. We do not hold your money in a trust or escrow account.

## 6. Failed, delayed or duplicate payments

If a payment fails, is delayed, or you believe you were charged twice, contact us with your order reference before retrying, so we can check the provider's record and avoid a duplicate charge. AgriBridge is not responsible for delays caused by your mobile money provider or bank, but we will help trace a payment using the provider's confirmation. We reconcile payments against the provider's record and do not create duplicate financial entries for the same payment.

## 7. Refunds

If an order is cancelled, or goods are not delivered or are not as described, you may be entitled to a refund or replacement. Refunds are reviewed **case by case** using the order records, the payment provider's confirmation and any evidence you provide. There is no automatic refund button yet; request a refund through the contact below with your order reference and photos where possible.

We do not exclude refunds that the law gives you. **Refund timing, and any deductions other than as the law allows, are to be confirmed** with a Ugandan advocate and the payment providers, and will be published in a new version.

## 8. Cancellations

You may cancel a pending order from your orders page. Once an order is confirmed or in transit, cancellation may not be possible or may be subject to conditions we will explain, because the seller may already have prepared or dispatched the goods. See the Delivery and Dispute Resolution Policy for what happens next.

## 9. Seller and transporter payouts

How and when a seller or transporter receives money depends on the payment method used for the order. With direct methods the buyer pays the seller directly. Where payment passes through AgriBridge's payment provider, settlement is recorded and any platform commission is shown before it is charged. Transporters are paid after delivery is confirmed, as set out in the Transporter Agreement. **Payout timelines: to be confirmed.**

## 10. Raising a payment problem

Contact us as soon as you can: quality or delivery complaints ideally within 48 hours of delivery (a guide, not a legal limit), and transporter payment disputes within 14 days of the delivery date as set out in the Transporter Agreement. We will review the evidence from both sides and try to resolve the matter fairly. If it is not resolved, you keep any remedy the law gives you, including the Ugandan courts and any applicable consumer or regulatory body. This policy does not impose mandatory arbitration.
